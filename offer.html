Legal information and offer

Effective from 13 November 2026 · https://indagor.online

This page defines the legal status of the information published on the website indagor.ru (the "Site"), including information about pricing and the scope of work on the Klondayk platform. It is addressed to Site visitors and prospective clients. This is an English courtesy translation: the Russian version at indagor.ru/offer/ is the legally binding text.

In short. All information on the Site, including any pricing indications, is for reference only and does not constitute a public offer (Article 437 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation). The final scope, timeline and price are agreed individually in the contract with the Client. The base package does not include AI modules, 2D floor plans or social media automation unless expressly agreed.

1. General provisions

The Site is owned and administered by the project INDAGOR (the "Contractor"). The Site is informational and describes the digital products, services and development approaches that the Contractor provides under separately concluded contracts.

Use of the Site means that the visitor has read this page and accepts the terms below to the extent applicable to the use of the Site's information.

2. Status of the information on the Site

In accordance with Article 437 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation, the information on the Site — including product descriptions, module lists, timelines, examples and prices — is an invitation to make offers and does not constitute a public offer.

Information on the Site does not oblige the Contractor to conclude a contract on the terms stated on the Site. The absence on the Site of the essential terms of a contract means that the offer cannot be accepted by acceptance within the meaning of Article 438 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

Descriptions of products and services are descriptive in nature and do not guarantee a specific commercial result (revenue, footfall, conversion, occupancy), since such a result depends on factors beyond the Contractor's control.

3. Price and scope of work

Pricing is quoted on request. The base pilot deployment is estimated per property as a starting reference figure. The final price is determined by the scope of work, the characteristics of the property and the number of modules and integrations.

The base pilot deployment includes:

  • the property website (public layer);
  • a Telegram bot for inquiries and notifications;
  • baseline performance analytics;
  • baseline SEO/GEO and analytics setup, plus corporate email on your domain (at the Client's option).

The final scope, volume, stages, timeline, payment schedule and price are fixed individually in the contract with the Client. Before the contract is signed, any information about price and timelines is indicative.

4. What is not included in the base package

Unless expressly agreed otherwise in the contract, the base price does not include:

  • AI modules, including the AI agent and the personal AI assistant;
  • 2D floor plans and the interactive scenarios related to them;
  • social media automation — publishing to VK, Telegram, Dzen and RUTUBE through official integrations, including the Orchestra module;
  • integrations with external systems (CRM, accounting systems, analytics services beyond the baseline setup);
  • advanced end-to-end analytics — end-to-end pipeline reporting beyond the baseline analytics setup;
  • content work beyond the agreed volume, photo and video production, advertising budgets;
  • subscriptions to third-party services and platforms required for certain modules to operate.

Additional modules are connected as needed; their price and timeline are discussed separately and fixed in the contract or an addendum to it.

5. How the contract is concluded

Work is performed under a contract concluded individually. Contacting us through the channel listed on the Site (Telegram) is a request for information or an invitation to negotiate and does not conclude a contract.

The contract is considered concluded upon signature by the parties or other acceptance expressly provided for by the contract (invoice, payment of an agreed stage). The terms of the contract prevail over any information published on the Site.
