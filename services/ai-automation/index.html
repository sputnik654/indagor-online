Use cases

What we automate

Each item is a separate scenario that can run on its own. It makes sense to start where manual work takes the most time.

01

AI assistants and chatbots

Answers to common enquiries, lead qualification, operator prompts. The assistant works from your knowledge base and hands complex cases to a human.

02

Document processing

Scan and PDF recognition, extraction of key fields and line items, rule-based validation and transfer into the accounting system with no manual entry.

03

CRM integrations

Data exchange between website, email, messengers and CRM: deal creation, status updates, notifications to the right owner at the right moment.

04

Parsing and data collection

Scheduled collection of information from public sources: prices, assortment, vacancies, mentions. Data is normalised and lands in a spreadsheet or database.

05

Routine operations

Reports, reconciliations, mailings, moving data between systems, generating documents from templates — anything that follows the same algorithm every time.

06

Quality control

Assistant and automation metrics: share of successful scenarios, handling time, errors. Action logs and the points where a human is required.

Questions and answers

What clients ask

Where does automation start?

With one process that consumes the most time and is described precisely enough to formalise. Then you need data access and one person who will confirm the result. We don't start by replacing systems: first we test the hypothesis on a narrow slice and measure the effect in hours and errors.

Do we have to replace our current CRM or accounting system?

In most cases, no. We connect to what you already use: we pull data via API or scheduled exports and push results back into the familiar system. Your team keeps working where it worked before. Replacing the platform is discussed separately, and only when the current one genuinely blocks the task.

How long does a pilot take?

A pilot is limited to one scenario — for example, automatic replies to common enquiries or data extraction from documents. That scope is enough to validate the hypothesis before full rollout and to see real numbers. The timeline depends on integration complexity and data quality; it is fixed in the work plan before the start.
