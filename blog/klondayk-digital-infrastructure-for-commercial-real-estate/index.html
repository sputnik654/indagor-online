The Shopping Center Wasn’t the Problem. Its Digital Infrastructure Was.

Published: 23 November 20269 min readINDAGOR

How INDAGOR created Klondayk — a digital platform designed to turn commercial real estate into a connected, measurable and revenue-oriented business system.

A business investigation by INDAGOR · Digital Products · AI Systems · Commercial Real Estate Technology

Imagine a shopping center with dozens of tenants, hundreds of daily interactions, vacant units, maintenance requests, promotions, customer inquiries and a management team responsible for keeping everything moving. From the outside, everything looks straightforward. But take a closer look.

Tenant information lives in spreadsheets. Leasing inquiries arrive through different channels. Unit availability is updated manually. Marketing campaigns operate separately from the leasing process. Reports are assembled from disconnected sources. Important decisions depend on information that may already be outdated.

The website presents the property. The management team manages the property. The marketing team promotes the property. But where is the system connecting all three?

That was the question behind Klondayk, an original commercial real estate platform developed by INDAGOR. And the answer led us somewhere much more interesting than building another website.

1. The investigation: a property is more than a collection of spaces

When we approached the problem of digitalizing commercial real estate, we deliberately avoided starting with a familiar question: What should the new website look like?

Instead, we asked: What should a shopping center be able to do through its digital infrastructure?

A modern shopping center has several interconnected business environments:

  • Visitors need to discover stores, services, events and offers.
  • Prospective tenants need to evaluate locations, available units and commercial opportunities.
  • Existing tenants need a reliable way to communicate with management.
  • Property managers need control over spaces, requests, operations and deadlines.
  • Owners need visibility into performance, demand and the commercial picture of the asset.

These are not five separate digital projects. They are different sides of the same business. Treating them as disconnected websites, spreadsheets, messengers and reporting tools creates unnecessary friction. We decided to approach the problem as a system architecture challenge.

2. The breakthrough: stop building pages. Connect business processes.

This became the central design principle of Klondayk by INDAGOR.

Klondayk is a proprietary digital platform for commercial real estate management, designed for shopping centers, business centers and other commercial properties. Its purpose is to connect the public-facing digital experience with the internal management environment. Instead of treating the website as the final product, we designed it as the entrance to a much larger digital ecosystem.

The architecture brings together three major layers:

  • Acquisition — attract attention and create demand. A premium property showcase, tenant catalog, search, categories, promotions, events and mobile-first visitor journeys. The goal is not simply to display information. It is to help visitors find what they need and turn interest into meaningful actions.
  • Conversion — turn interest into commercial opportunities. Available units, property information, inquiry forms, lead capture, qualification and structured deal handling. A prospective tenant should not have to navigate an unclear process just to express interest in a commercial space.
  • Management — give the team control. An administrative environment for property data, tenants, units, requests, content, permissions, notifications and reporting. This is where scattered information becomes a connected operational picture.

One architecture. Three interconnected business functions.

3. Inside Klondayk: what makes the platform different?

The key difference is not a particular screen or visual effect. It is the relationship between the modules.

A. Tenant and property management

The platform is designed to bring essential property information into one structured environment: tenant records and contact information, occupied and vacant units, floors, areas and property structure, tenant-to-unit relationships, and interaction history. Instead of searching through multiple spreadsheets, the management team can work with connected records.

B. Leasing as a managed business pipeline

A vacant commercial unit is not simply an empty space. It is a commercial opportunity that needs to be presented, discovered, evaluated and processed. Klondayk connects the public presentation of available units with inquiry capture and internal handling.

The commercial workflow includes unit information, inquiry registration, notifications to responsible employees, deal statuses and priorities, interaction history, Kanban-style pipeline management, and data export.

This changes the logic of leasing. Rather than merely publishing a phone number and waiting for someone to call, the property gains a structured way to manage demand.

C. A management dashboard, not another spreadsheet

A digital platform becomes valuable when it helps people make decisions. Klondayk is designed around a unified administrative environment where property data, tenant information, inquiries, content and operational activity can be connected. The objective is simple: less manual reconciliation, more visibility, clearer responsibility, better-informed decisions.

D. Analytics that connects marketing with commercial activity

This is one of the most important parts of the concept. Traditional website statistics often stop at page views and traffic sources. But a property owner needs more meaningful answers: Where does audience attention come from? Which tenant categories attract interest? Which units generate inquiries? Which marketing channels bring potential customers? Where are opportunities being lost?

Klondayk’s analytics architecture is designed to connect traffic sources, audience behavior, inquiries and leasing activity. The idea is to move from simply counting visits to understanding what those visits can mean for the business.

E. AI as a business interface

We did not want AI to be just another decorative chatbot. The planned AI layer is designed to work with the platform’s business context. Depending on the connected modules and permissions, an AI assistant can help users ask questions about property information in natural language, summarize units, prepare emails, and retrieve relevant information. Business rules and critical operations remain controlled by the platform’s server-side logic.

The AI module is an optional extension, not part of the base deployment. Useful AI must be connected to real business data and processes, not added merely for appearance.

4. The part many developers overlook: a platform should help create commercial value

A digital product should not be evaluated only by how much it costs to build. It should also be evaluated by the opportunities it creates. For commercial real estate, those opportunities begin with several practical mechanisms:

First: better presentation of available space. Second: more organized demand handling. Third: marketing that works with the property. Fourth: measurable marketing activity. Fifth: additional opportunities through digital services (tenant promotion, sponsored placements).

These are not guaranteed revenue streams. They are opportunities that can be designed, tested and measured. We do not believe that installing software automatically creates profit. We believe that properly designed digital infrastructure can make commercial opportunities easier to discover, manage, measure and develop. The platform should not only support the property. It should help the property work more effectively as a business.

5. Marketing 360°: the digital property should not end at its website

A shopping center competes for attention every day. Its digital presence needs to extend beyond a homepage. The Klondayk concept includes a broader marketing environment: property promotions, tenant presentation, content planning, social platform communication, and campaign performance analytics.

An optional social publishing module, Orchestra, is designed to connect content preparation and approved publishing workflows across major global platforms, including LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, and Telegram. It is about connecting the property’s marketing activity with its tenant ecosystem and commercial objectives.

6. One platform does not mean one rigid template

Commercial properties are different. Klondayk follows a modular architecture. The configuration can be shaped around the property’s structure, tenant management requirements, leasing workflows, operational processes, and reporting needs. The system is designed to evolve rather than force every property into the same predefined configuration. We are building a digital foundation that can adapt to the property.

7. From concept to a structured deployment model

Klondayk is organized into a base deployment and additional modules. The base pilot package includes: a property website, tenant catalog, Telegram bot for inquiries, baseline traffic analytics, SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) readiness, web analytics and tag management connected, and property-specific launch. The platform architecture is designed with data privacy and global compliance standards in mind.

Additional capabilities can be connected separately: AI agent, interactive 2D floor plans, advanced end-to-end analytics, and social media automation. The exact scope depends on the property.

This modular approach makes it possible to start with a practical foundation and expand the system as business needs develop. Build the right foundation first. Expand with purpose.

8. Why INDAGOR built Klondayk

Klondayk reflects a broader philosophy behind INDAGOR — Intelligent Digital Systems. We believe technology creates the greatest value when it connects things that previously operated separately. A website should be more than a collection of pages. An AI assistant should be more than a chat window. Analytics should be more than a dashboard full of numbers. Each component should contribute to a coherent business system.

That principle guides our work across digital product development, custom software, AI-powered automation, CRM integrations and management analytics.

9. The next chapter: commercial real estate as an intelligent digital asset

We see several directions for the future development of Klondayk: Visitor Intelligence, Leasing Intelligence, and Owner Intelligence. The ambition is to create an environment where information becomes more connected, processes become more transparent and management decisions can be supported by better data.

The conclusion: the real product is not the website

A shopping center does not need another digital brochure. It needs a digital environment that connects visitors, tenants, available spaces, management, marketing and analytics. That is the idea behind Klondayk. And that is what INDAGOR set out to build.

We are interested in working with shopping center owners, business center operators, commercial property developers and management companies who see digital infrastructure as a long-term business asset.

Frequently asked questions

What is Klondayk?

Klondayk is a proprietary digital platform developed by INDAGOR for commercial real estate management. It connects public-facing property presentation, tenant and unit management, leasing workflows, marketing, and analytics into one unified system.

Is Klondayk just another website builder?

No. Klondayk is designed as a digital infrastructure. While it includes a premium public-facing website, its core value lies in the internal management environment, connecting leasing pipelines, operational tasks, and marketing analytics.

How does Klondayk use AI?

The AI layer in Klondayk is designed as a business interface, not a decorative chatbot. It helps management teams retrieve property data, summarize inquiries, and draft content based on real business context and user permissions.

Does Klondayk automatically generate profit?

No. INDAGOR believes software alone does not create profit. However, Klondayk provides the infrastructure that makes commercial opportunities easier to discover, manage, measure, and develop through structured workflows.

Can Klondayk be customized for different properties?

Yes. Klondayk follows a modular architecture. It can be configured based on the property’s size, specific management requirements, and desired level of automation.
