Privacy Policy

Effective from 11 November 2026 · https://indagor.online

This Policy describes what data is processed when you visit the website indagor.ru (the "Site"), for what purpose, on what legal basis and what rights the visitor has. The Policy is drawn up in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 "On Personal Data". This is an English courtesy translation: the Russian version at indagor.ru/privacy.html is the legally binding text.

1. Operator

The Site is owned and administered by the project INDAGOR (digital engineering studio). The personal data operator is INDAGOR.

Contact on any matter related to data processing: Telegram — t.me/INDAGOR_CEO. Telegram and email are the official contact channels listed on the Site.

2. What data is processed

The Site contains no registration forms, enquiry forms or input fields. The Operator does not request or store names, phone numbers, email addresses or other identifying information on the Site. When any website is accessed, technical data is transmitted automatically; this Site does not store it, set cookies or assign identifiers. Technically transmitted data:

  • IP address (processed by the hosting provider in standard operation, not stored by the Operator);
  • browser, device, screen resolution and language;
  • date and time of access, requested page address, previous page address (referrer);
  • no cookie identifiers and no behavioural data are collected.

This data does not allow the identity of the visitor to be established and is not used for decisions producing legal consequences.

If a visitor contacts us directly via Telegram or email, the Operator receives the data the visitor voluntarily provides: name or nickname, message text, attachments. Such data is used solely to respond to the enquiry.

3. Purposes of processing

  • ensuring the operability and security of the Site;
  • protecting the Site from abuse and technical faults;
  • improving the structure, content and usability of the Site;
  • responding to enquiries received through the listed contact channels.

The data is not used for marketing mailings or profiling with legal consequences and is not sold to third parties.

4. Legal bases

Traffic on indagor.online is not measured with third-party analytics: the site sets no cookies, loads no trackers and does not identify visitors. Processing is limited to what is necessary to serve the pages (Article 6 of 152-FZ) and, where a visitor writes to us, to the data voluntarily provided in that message, processed on the basis of consent expressed by sending it.

5. Cookies and analytics services

Cookies are small text files stored in the browser. They are used for the correct operation of the Site and for statistics. A visitor may delete stored cookies at any time and block their storage in the browser settings; some elements of the Site may then behave differently.

These services are operated by third parties: Yandex LLC (Russian Federation) and Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited (USA, Ireland). Data processing by these services is governed by their own privacy policies. When Google services are used, anonymised technical data may be transferred across borders outside the Russian Federation.

The proprietary INDAGOR counter works without cookies: the tab identifier is kept only in browser memory (sessionStorage) and disappears when the tab is closed. The IP address is not transmitted or stored, and the full User-Agent is not recorded — only the device type. Events are stored in Netlify Blobs (Netlify, Inc., USA) in anonymised form; they contain no personal data and cannot be linked to a specific visitor.

You can disable cookies in your browser or use private browsing at any time. This site does not set cookies, so its behaviour will not change.

6. Retention periods

No statistical reports are kept for this site. Correspondence data is stored until the purpose of the enquiry is achieved or consent is withdrawn.

7. Transfer of data to third parties

The Operator does not transfer data to third parties, except in cases expressly provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation (a request from an authorised body).

8. Rights of the visitor

In accordance with Articles 14–16 of 152-FZ, the visitor has the right to:

  • obtain information about the processing of their personal data;
  • request that data be clarified, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or obtained unlawfully;
  • withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • appeal the actions or inaction of the operator to Roskomnadzor or in court.

Requests are sent via the Telegram channel listed in section 1. The review period is no more than 30 days from receipt of the request.

9. Security

The Site transmits data over the secure HTTPS protocol. Access to administrative tools is restricted. The Operator takes organisational and technical measures to protect data from unauthorised access, alteration and distribution.

10. Changes to the Policy

The Policy may be updated. The current version is always available at indagor.ru/privacy.html; the effective date is stated at the top of the page. Continued use of the Site after changes are published means acceptance of the new version.
